Currency

Applies to marketplace checkout, hosting plans, and Paystack/Stripe transactions. Prices you enter anywhere in the admin (products, invoices, payment links) are in this currency unless you set a different one on that specific item.

Exchange Rates

Enter how many units of your Site Currency ({{ sv('site_currency') or 'NGN' }}) equal 1 unit of each currency below — e.g. if 1 USD = ₦1,500 today, put 1500 in the USD row. These are set by you, not auto-fetched, so they stay exactly what you intend until you change them. A currency left blank won't appear in the site-wide currency switcher, and any existing revenue in it will be counted 1:1 (unconverted) in Overview/Analytics until you set a rate.
